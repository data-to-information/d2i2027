Arianna’s Thread

Given a noisy DNA fragment, retrieve its closest matches from a large reference collection — with as few bits per sequence as possible, and fast.

The challenge connects three levels: representing and compressing sequences into finite-bit codes, searching those codes, and accelerating the complete retrieval pipeline on GPUs.
